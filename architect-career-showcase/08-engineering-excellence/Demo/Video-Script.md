## Interview Discussion

### Why this approach?

A timed script prevents over-demoing stubs.

### Alternative approaches

Unscripted 30 min wander. Loses audience.

### Trade-offs

Must re-record when UI changes.

### Enterprise adoption

Add captions; chapter markers.

### Scaling considerations

Version videos with release tags.

### Principal Architect interview questions

**Q1. Ideal length?**  
Under 10 minutes for async review.

**Q2. Key line to land?**  
Browser never talks to AI Platform.
